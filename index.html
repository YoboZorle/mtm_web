Who we are

A Christ-centred ministry

Manifestation Time Ministries is a Christ-centred Christian ministry committed to spreading the teachings and love of Jesus Christ.

Through the preaching and teaching of God's Word and the ministry of the Holy Spirit, we seek to bring people into the freedom, purpose and fullness available in Christ Jesus. Our desire is to see lives transformed, destinies restored and believers empowered to fulfil their God-given purposes.

Our mandate

Raised and empowered for a divine assignment.

God has commissioned this ministry to declare His people free from all forms of satanic afflictions, oppressions and bondage, and empower them to manifest the fullness of their potentials in Christ Jesus through the Word and Spirit.

Our mandate is to preach the Gospel of freedom, teach the uncompromising Word of God and minister through the power of the Holy Spirit, bringing people into the liberty and purpose available in Christ.

Romans 8:19–211 John 3:8
Who we are

Vision, mission and core values

To see God's people liberated from every form of satanic bondage and empowered to manifest their divine destinies and full potentials in Christ Jesus.

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, set captives free through the power of God's Word and Spirit, and equip believers to discover, develop and manifest their God-given potentials and purposes.

The Word of God

We uphold the Word of God as the foundation of our faith, teaching and Christian living.

The power of the Holy Spirit

We depend on the Holy Spirit for spiritual empowerment, transformation and the manifestation of God's purpose.

Freedom and deliverance

We believe in the victory of Jesus Christ over the works of darkness and the freedom available to everyone who believes in Him.

Divine purpose

We believe every individual has a God-given purpose and potential to discover and fulfil in Christ.

Faith and prayer

We cultivate a life of faith, prayer and dependence on God.

Love and service

We are committed to demonstrating the love of Christ through fellowship, service and reaching others with the Gospel.

Scriptural foundation

The Word behind our name

Our mandate rests on these scriptures (King James Version).

“For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God.”

“For the creature was made subject to vanity, not willingly, but by reason of him who hath subjected the same in hope,”

“Because the creature itself also shall be delivered from the bondage of corruption into the glorious liberty of the children of God.”

“He that committeth sin is of the devil; for the devil sinneth from the beginning. For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that he might destroy the works of the devil.”

Privacy policy

How Manifestation Time Ministries handles the information you share through this website.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us in our forms: for example your name, phone number, email address and the details of a prayer request, membership application, enquiry or gift.

How we use it

First-timer details go to our follow-up team. Membership applications go to the membership team for review. Prayer and counselling requests are seen only by authorised prayer and counselling personnel. We send service and event updates only if you tick the separate updates box.

Payments

Online gifts and shop orders are processed by a secure payment provider. We do not store card details.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by contacting the church office through the Contact page.

Content on this website, including messages, images and branding, belongs to Manifestation Time Ministries and may not be reused commercially without permission. Shop prices and availability may change. Gifts are voluntary contributions to the work of the ministry.
